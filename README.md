# Soft Skies

Soft Skies is a relaxing low-poly flight sim about drifting through bright open skies, gentle terrain, and quiet procedural landscapes.

Take flight as a colorful phoenix or Chinese-inspired dragon and explore open, ever-unfolding maps of plains, mountains, rivers, jungles, rainforests, waterfalls, deserts, oases, stone forests, snowy temple peaks, villages, clouds, and shifting daylight. There are no scores, timers, or combat - just smooth free-flight, soft scenery, and a calm place to wander.
